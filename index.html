Our programs

Three free, mission-driven programs serving public health, children, and literacy.

📻

Universal Health Radio

Public-health media and audio broadcasting that translate trustworthy health guidance from WHO, CDC, and FDA into dozens of languages — free to listen, anywhere.

📚

katha.kids

Children's stories and educational content that make learning joyful and accessible for kids everywhere, regardless of means.

🕯️

noorjyoti

A reading and literacy program bringing books and the light of learning to readers who need it most.

Our mission

We believe access to health knowledge and education is a public good, not a privilege. Universal Health Foundation builds and gives away tools that inform, teach, and uplift — funded by donations and grants, free at the point of use.
